Questions

DTS writes the questions. Until you open the assessment, you can fix the wording of any question and add your own at the end. Once it is open, the questions are fixed, so that everyone answers the same ones.

Open it from Questions in the menu on the left. Each question shows how people answer it (Pick one, Pick any, Scale of 1 to 5 or Written answer), its choices, and “(required)” if people must answer it.

What the questions cover

The standard assessment has about two dozen questions in five sections. DTS may tailor them for your church, so yours can differ a little.

SectionWhat it asks
About youThe person’s role at the church, and the ministry areas they work or serve in.
How you use AI todayHow often they use AI tools, which ones, what for in church work, whose account they use, what kinds of information they have put into one, and a story of AI helping or going wrong.
How you would like to use AIHow they feel about the church using AI, and where it would help them most.
How comfortable would you be with AI helping with each of these?A 1-to-5 scale for tasks such as drafting emails, preparing sermons and lessons, creating images or music, a chatbot for guests, summarizing prayer requests or care notes, and analyzing giving and attendance.
Concerns and guardrailsWhat worries them, whether the church should say when AI helped, what AI should never be used for, how sure they are about what is okay today, what would help, and anything else for leadership.

To see the assessment exactly as staff will, press Preview as staff see it at the top of the Questions page. It opens in a new tab. The preview only works for you and DTS, and answers sent from it are not saved.

Rewording a question

You can do this only while the assessment is Not open yet.

  1. Press Edit next to the question.
  2. Change the Question, the Helper line under the question (optional), or the Choices, one per line. For a scale, change What 1 means and What 5 means.
  3. Under Options, tick or untick People must answer this question and, for a question with choices, Let people type their own answer ("Other").
  4. Press Save question. You see “Saved”.

For a question DTS wrote, How people answer is greyed out: “Set by DTS. You can change the wording and the choices.” The way a question is answered shapes how DTS reads the results, so only DTS can change it.

A question with choices needs at least two. If you see “Give at least two choices.”, add another choice or tick the “Other” box.

Adding your own questions

Ask anything your church wants to know that the standard questions do not cover. Your questions go at the end, in a section named “From” and your church’s name.

  1. Scroll to Your own questions at the bottom of the page.
  2. Press Add a question.
  3. Type the Question, and a helper line if you want one.
  4. Choose How people answer: Pick one, Pick any, Scale of 1 to 5 or Written answer.
  5. For Pick one or Pick any, type the Choices, one per line. For a scale, say what 1 and 5 mean.
  6. Tick the Options you want, and press Add question.

A church can add up to 15 questions of its own.

Removing one of your own questions

  1. Find the question in your church’s section at the end.
  2. Press the bin icon (Remove question) next to Edit.
  3. Confirm. You see “Question removed”.

Only questions your church added can be removed. To drop one of DTS’s questions, ask the DTS person working with your church.

Why the questions lock when it opens

Once you open the assessment, the Questions page says: “The assessment has been opened, so the questions are fixed. If something needs to change, ask your DTS contact.” The Edit buttons and Your own questions disappear, and the button at the top becomes Open the assessment page.

This keeps the results fair: if a question changed after some people had answered, their answers would mean something different from everyone else’s. The questions stay fixed even if you close the assessment later, and it cannot go back to “Not open yet”.

So check the questions before you press Open the assessment. Use Preview as staff see it to read them through once.
