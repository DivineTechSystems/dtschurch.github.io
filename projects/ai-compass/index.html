AI Compass

Guidelines for how your church uses AI

AI Compass helps your church write its own AI guidelines: what is fine, what needs care, and what you will not do. DivineTech (DTS) writes them with you from two things: conversations with your leadership, and a short staff assessment that your church runs itself.

I’m a Church Admin I got an email asking me to take it

The two parts

DTS runs this

Leadership conversations

DTS meets with your church’s leaders to understand how you work, what you care about, and where AI could help or hurt. DTS handles this part from start to finish, including writing up what was said. You do not need to do anything in AI Compass for it.

Your church runs this

The staff assessment

A short set of questions for your staff about how they use AI today, how they feel about it, and what worries them. It takes about 8 minutes. Your Church Admins send it out and see the results. Staff do not need an account.

DTS puts both together and drafts your church’s AI guidelines with you.

Who does what

WhoWhat they do
DTSSets up AI Compass for your church and gives your Church Admins their sign-in. Runs the leadership conversations. Sets whether the staff assessment is anonymous or tracked by person. Drafts the guidelines.
Church Admin
one or more of your church’s AI leads		Signs in to AI Compass. Builds the staff list, checks the questions, opens the assessment, emails the link, sends reminders, closes it, and reads the results.
StaffGet an email with a link, answer the questions, and press Send my answers. No account or password.

The whole job, in order

For a Church Admin, the staff assessment goes like this:

Staff list → check the questions → open → send the link → reminders → close → results → DTS drafts the guidelines
