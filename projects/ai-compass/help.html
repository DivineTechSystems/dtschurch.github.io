Questions and help

Answers to the questions people ask most, first for Church Admins, then for staff. At the bottom: who to ask when this page does not cover it.

For Church Admins

Someone didn’t get the email

  1. On Staff list, check their status. Not sent means it has not gone yet: press Send.
  2. If it says Link sent, press Edit and check their email address for a typo. Fix it and press Save, then Send again.
  3. Ask them to look in their junk or spam folder for an email from your church’s name “via AI Compass”.
  4. Still nothing? For a tracked assessment, press Get link and send them the link yourself. For an anonymous one, press Copy link by “Your church’s link” and send them that.

If an email could not be sent, the message after sending names the person and says why.

Can I see who answered what?

It depends on how DTS set up the assessment.

  • Anonymous: no. No name is kept with any answer. You can read each response on Results, but with no name, and you cannot see who has answered.
  • Tracked by person: yes. Staff list shows who has answered, and Results, By person shows each person’s answers with their name.

Only DTS can switch an assessment between anonymous and tracked. See Anonymous or tracked.

Can I change a question after it opened?

No. Once the assessment is open, the questions are fixed, so that everyone answers the same ones. They stay fixed after you close it, and it cannot go back to “Not open yet”. If something really needs to change, ask the DTS person working with your church. See Why the questions lock.

Someone left the church

Press Edit on their row, untick On the list, and press Save. They get no more links, and a personal link they have stops working. Anything they already answered stays in the results. See Taking someone off the list.

The link says closed

Someone closed the assessment (you or another Church Admin). If you want more answers, press Reopen the assessment on Staff list. See Closing and reopening.

Can I add people after it opened?

Yes. Add them as usual, then press Send on their row, or the Email the N … button.

The send buttons are greyed out

  • The assessment is not open yet, or is closed. The People section says which. Open or reopen it first.
  • Everyone already has it: the big button says Everyone has been sent it or Everyone has answered. Use Send again on a row instead.
  • The page says “Email is not set up yet, so links cannot be sent from here.” Ask the DTS person working with your church. Meanwhile share the link yourself (see above).

Can someone answer twice?

With a tracked assessment, no: each personal link works once, and if you send someone a new link and they answer again, it replaces what they sent before. With an anonymous one, anyone with the church link can answer, and AI Compass cannot tell who has already answered, so share it only with your staff.

Can I delete answers, such as test answers?

Not from your side. If you tried the assessment out and want test answers removed, ask the DTS person working with your church. Use Preview as staff see it on Questions to look at the assessment without saving answers.

Are there limits?

A staff list holds up to 500 people. A church can add up to 15 questions of its own. The email subject can be up to 150 characters and the message up to 4,000.

I forgot my password

There is no reset link. Ask the DTS person who invited you to set a new one, then change it under your name at the bottom of the menu, Change password.

For staff

The link says “This survey has closed”

Your church has stopped taking answers. If you still want to answer, ask your Church Admin. They can reopen it.

The link says it is not active

The link may be mistyped, not open yet, or replaced by a newer one (each new personal link turns off the one before). Ask your Church Admin to send you a new one.

I lost the email

Ask your Church Admin to send it again. If your church uses personal links, you can also open your church’s link, type your email address, and tap Send my link. If you are on the list and have not answered yet, your link arrives within a minute or two. Check your junk folder.

Can I change my answers?

Not once they are sent. With a personal link, your Church Admin can send you a new one, and what you send from it replaces your earlier answers.

Several of us are using one computer

With the shared church link, after one person sends their answers the page shows “Answers sent”. Tap Answer again as someone else to start fresh for the next person.

It says “Too many responses from this network right now”

A lot of people have answered from the same internet connection (for example the church wifi) in the last hour. Wait a while and try again, or use your phone’s data.

Is it really anonymous?

If your email said “Your answers are anonymous”, yes: your name is not kept with your answers. If it said “This link is yours alone”, your name is kept. See Who sees your answers.

Who to ask

Staff

Ask your Church Admin

The person at your church leading this, for example Pastor Jordan. They can resend your link, fix your email address, and answer questions about the assessment.

Church Admins

Ask the DTS person who invited you
