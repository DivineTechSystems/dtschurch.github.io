AI Compass

For Church Admins

You are one of your church’s AI leads. In AI Compass you get the staff assessment to your people and see what they said. This page covers signing in, what you will see, and the whole job in order.

How you get access

DTS sets up your account. The DTS person working with your church creates it with your email address and a starting password, and gives you your church’s assessment. They will tell you your sign-in details themselves: AI Compass does not send a separate invitation email.

When DTS gives you the assessment, you are also put on its staff list. You are staff too, so you will get the assessment like everyone else.

Signing in

  1. Go to compass.dtschurch.com.
  2. Type your Email and Password.
  3. Press Sign in. You land on Home.

Church Admins sign in with an email address and password. There is no “Sign in with Microsoft” for church accounts.

If you seeWhat to do
Email or password is incorrect.Check both and try again. Passwords are case-sensitive.
Too many attempts. Try again in 15 minutes.After 8 wrong tries, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes. Wait, then try again.
Sign in at … instead.Your password was right, but you are on the wrong address. Go to the address it names.

Changing your password

  1. At the bottom of the menu on the left, press your name.
  2. Choose Change password.
  3. Type your Current password and a New password of at least 10 characters.
  4. Press Change password. You see “Password changed”, and any other browser signed in as you is signed out.

Change the starting password DTS gave you the first time you sign in.

Forgot your password? There is no reset link. Ask the DTS person who invited you to set a new one.

Home

Home has a card for your church’s assessment. It shows:

  • The assessment’s title and whether it is Not open yet, Open or Closed.
  • Whether answers are anonymous or tracked by person.
  • What to do next, with a button: Add your staff, Open the assessment, Send the link or See the results.
  • How many people are On your staff list (and how many were sent the link), how many Responses have come in, and a shortcut to Questions.

Only your church

You see only your own church, and only the assessments DTS has given you. If DTS has not given you one yet, Home says “Nothing is ready for you yet. Your DTS contact will let you know when it is.” If you open a link to something you do not have, you see “This page is not available to you.”

If your church has more than one assessment (for example, one this year and one next year), Home shows a card for each. Pick one, and the menu’s Staff list, Results and Questions follow it. Each assessment has its own staff list.

Your checklist

  1. Add your staff to the list, one at a time or from a file.
  2. Check the questions. Fix any wording and add your own at the end.
  3. Check the email staff will get, and send yourself a test.
  4. Open the assessment. Nothing is emailed yet.
  5. Send the link to everyone on the list.
  6. Send reminders to people who still need it.
  7. Close the assessment when you have enough answers.
  8. Read the results and talk them over with DTS.

More than one Church Admin

A church can have several Church Admins. Everyone DTS gives the same assessment works on the same thing:

  • the same staff list,
  • the same questions,
  • the same email wording,
  • the same open or closed setting,
  • the same results.

A change one of you makes is there for the others. If someone else is working at the same time, reload the page to see their changes.

Each of you has your own email and password. When you send the link, replies to that email come to you, not to the other Church Admins.
