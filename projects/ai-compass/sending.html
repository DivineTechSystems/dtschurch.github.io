Sending and reminders

Everything on this page happens on the Staff list page. The box at the top says whether the assessment is open and has the button to open or close it. The People section below has the email and the send buttons.

Anonymous or tracked

DTS sets up each assessment one of two ways. The Staff list page and the Home card tell you which yours is. To change it, ask the DTS person working with your church.

AnonymousTracked by person
The linkEveryone gets the same church link.Each person gets their own link, which works once.
Names with answersNo name is kept with any answer.The person’s name is kept with their answers. DTS can see them.
What you can seeWho was sent the link, but not who answered.Who was sent the link, who has answered (with the day), and what they said.
ResultsEveryone together, and each response on its own with no name.Everyone together, and each person’s answers with their name. See Results.
“Your church’s link”Anyone with it can answer, so you can also share it yourself, in a staff email or meeting.It takes no answers. Someone who has lost their email can open it, type their email address, and get their own link again.

If DTS switches an assessment between anonymous and tracked after links have gone out, those links stop counting and everyone shows as Not sent again. Send the link again afterwards.

Opening the assessment

Staff cannot answer until you open it. Open it when your list and the questions are ready.

  1. Go to Staff list.
  2. In the box that says “The assessment is not open yet”, press Open the assessment.
  3. Confirm. The message reminds you that staff can then answer it, the questions can no longer be changed, and nothing is emailed until you send the link.

You see “The assessment is open”. The box now shows Your church’s link with a Copy link button, and the send buttons in People come on.

Rewording the email

Staff get an email from your church’s name “via AI Compass”. If they reply, the reply comes to whoever sent it: you, when you press send. You can reword the subject and message at any time; the same email is used whether you send to one person or the whole list.

  1. In People, press Edit the email. The box “The email staff get” opens.
  2. Change the Subject (up to 150 characters) and the Message (up to 4,000 characters).
  3. Press Send me a test to get the email yourself. Its subject starts with “Test:”.
  4. Press Save. You see “Email saved”.

Use the standard wording puts back the wording AI Compass starts with: the subject is the assessment’s title and your church’s name, and the message says your church is working on AI guidelines and that it takes about 8 minutes.

Words in braces

Write these in the subject or message and each person’s email fills them in:

WriteBecomes
{first_name}The person’s first name, from the staff list (or “there” if there is none).
{name}Their whole name as it is on the list.
{church}Your church’s name.
{link}Where the button goes. Message only, and only once.

Anything else in braces is refused when you save, with a message that says which words you can use. {link} cannot go in the subject.

Added every time

AI Compass adds these to every email, so you do not need to write them:

  • A sentence saying whether answers are anonymous. For an anonymous assessment: “Your answers are anonymous. Everyone at your church gets this same link, and your name is not kept with what you say.” For a tracked one: “This link is yours alone and works once. Your name is kept with your answers, so please do not forward it.” You cannot change this sentence, so the email always matches how the assessment really works.
  • A button with the assessment’s title that opens the link, and the link written out in case the button does not work.
  • A footer: “Sent for” your church’s name “by DivineTech’s AI Compass.”

These go after your message. To put the button somewhere else, such as above your sign-off, write {link} on a line of its own there.

Sending the link

The assessment must be open. Before that, the People section says “The assessment is not open yet. Links can be sent once it opens.”

To everyone who needs it

  1. In People, press the blue button on the right. It says Email the N not yet sent it (anonymous) or Email the N who have not answered (tracked).
  2. Confirm. AI Compass emails each of them.

You see “Sent to N people”, and their status changes to Link sent. If some emails could not be sent, the message names those people and says why.

To one person

Press Send on their row. If they have been sent it before, the button says Send again.

Sharing the link yourself

For an anonymous assessment you can also press Copy link next to Your church’s link and share it however you like. Anyone with it can answer, so share it only with your staff.

If email is not set up for your church yet, the page says so and the send buttons stay off. Share your church’s link yourself (anonymous), use Get link for each person (tracked), or ask the DTS person working with your church.

Reminders

There is no automatic reminder. To chase people, send again.

  • Tracked: press Email the N who have not answered. Only people who have not answered yet get it. Each gets a fresh link, and their earlier link stops working. When everyone has answered, the button says Everyone has answered.
  • Anonymous: AI Compass cannot tell who has answered, so the big button only reaches people never sent the link. When everyone has been sent it, it says Everyone has been sent it. To remind everyone, press Send again on each row, or send your church’s link in your own staff email. People who already answered can ignore it.

You can reword the email before a reminder (for example “A last reminder: …”) with Edit the email.

For a tracked assessment, if you press Send again for someone who has already answered, AI Compass warns you first. If they answer again, it replaces what they sent before.

Giving someone their link by hand (tracked only)

If someone’s email does not arrive, you can make a link and pass it on yourself, for example in a text message or a chat.

  1. Press Get link on their row.
  2. Confirm. Any link they were sent before stops working.
  3. The box “Link for” and their name shows the new link. Press Copy link, then Done.
  4. Send it to that person only.

The link is shown this one time. If you lose it, press Get link again. Their status stays as it was until they answer.

Closing and reopening

Close

  1. On Staff list, press Close the assessment.
  2. Confirm. You see “The assessment is closed”.

The link stops taking answers: anyone who opens it sees “This survey has closed. Thank you for your interest.” Answers already sent are kept, and links can no longer be sent.

Reopen

Changed your mind, or someone still wants to answer? Press Reopen the assessment. The link takes answers again, including personal links already sent that have not been used. The questions stay fixed.
