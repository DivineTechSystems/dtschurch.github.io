Results

Open Results in the menu on the left to see what your staff said. Answers show up as they come in, from the first one. Reload the page to see the newest.

At the top of the page, pick how to look at them:

  • Everyone together: all the answers added up, question by question.
  • By person (tracked assessment) or Each response (anonymous assessment): one person’s answers at a time.

Until someone answers, the page says “No one has answered yet. Results show up here as people send their answers.” Results stay here after you close the assessment.

Everyone together

The top line says how many responses there are and the day the latest one came in. No names are shown with answers in this view. Under it, each section of the assessment is listed with its questions:

Kind of questionWhat you see
Pick one, Pick anyA bar for each choice, with how many picked it and the percentage. For Pick any, people could pick several, so the numbers add up to more than the people who answered. What people typed under “Other” is listed under the bars.
Scale of 1 to 5The average out of 5, and a bar for each number from 1 to 5.
Written answerEvery answer, word for word. The first eight are shown; press Show all for the rest.

Each question also says how many people answered it (for example “12 of 15 answered”), because not every question is required.

Show answers from

Use Show answers from at the top to see only some people’s answers, for example only staff in one role, or only people who gave one answer to “How often do you use AI tools”. Pick Everyone to go back to all of them. The top line then says how many responses match, such as “6 of 15 responses”.

One response at a time

Tracked assessment (By person): the page says how many people have answered and lists them by name. Press a name to open that person’s answers, section by section.

Anonymous assessment (Each response): the responses are numbered (Response 1, Response 2, …) in no particular order, with no names. Each one is one person’s answers. The numbers do not tell you who answered first.

Under a question, “No answer” means the person skipped it.

Handle answers with care. Staff were told how their answers would be used. In an anonymous assessment, please do not try to work out who wrote what, even when you think you can tell. Answers that people feel safe giving are what make the guidelines useful.

What happens next

DTS sees the same answers. DTS puts them together with what your leaders said in their conversations with DTS and drafts your church’s AI guidelines with you. You do not need to export or send anything.

If you want to talk about what the results mean for your church, speak to the DTS person working with you.
