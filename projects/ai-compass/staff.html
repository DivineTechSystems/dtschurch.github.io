AI Compass · For staff

Got an email about AI Compass?

Your church is writing guidelines for how it uses AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot, with help from DivineTech. Your answers help shape them.

  • From: your church’s name “via AI Compass”. It was sent by one of your church’s AI leads (your Church Admin).
  • How long: about 8 minutes.
  • What you need: nothing. No account, no password. Just the button in the email.
  • Right answers: there are none. Say what you really think.

How to answer

  1. In the email, tap the button with the assessment’s name. (If it does not work, copy the address under it into your browser.)
  2. Answer the questions. Ones marked “(required)” must be answered; you can skip the rest.
  3. At the bottom, tap Send my answers.
  4. You see Answers sent. That’s it.

The bar at the top shows how many you have answered. If the page reloads or your connection drops, your answers so far are kept on that device.

Who sees your answers

It depends on the kind of link you got. The email tells you which.

“Your answers are anonymous”

Everyone at your church got the same link.

  • Your name, email address, device and internet address are not kept with your answers. Only the day you answered is.
  • Your Church Admins can see that you were sent the link, but not whether you answered.
  • Your Church Admins and DivineTech can read the answers, with no names on them.
  • Written answers are shown word for word. If you want to stay anonymous, leave out details that would point to you.

“This link is yours alone and works once”

You got your own link.

  • Your name is kept with your answers.
  • Your Church Admins and DivineTech can see that you answered and what you said.
  • The link works once. Please do not forward it.

Questions?

Ask your Church Admin, the person at your church leading this (for example, Pastor Jordan). They can resend your link, fix your email address, or answer questions about the assessment. More answers are on Questions and help.
