Billing

The Billing page shows your plan, the card on file, your next payment and every invoice. Payments are handled by Stripe; Bedrock never sees or stores your card number.

The four tiles

TileWhat it shows
PlanYour Bedrock plan and what it costs each month.
StatusActive when everything is in order. Payment past due or Unpaid means a payment did not go through.
Next paymentThe date of the next charge. It is always the first of the month.
Card on fileThe kind of card, its last four digits and when it expires.

The note at the bottom of the page says when these figures were last updated from Stripe.

Changing the card

  1. Press Change payment method. Stripe’s secure billing page opens for your church.
  2. Enter the new card and save.
  3. Stripe brings you back to Bedrock.

Manage billing opens the same Stripe page with everything on it: your card, your billing details and your past invoices.

Receipts go to the billing email shown under the buttons. To change that address, use Manage billing or ask DTS.

Invoices

Every invoice is listed with its number, date, the period it covers, whether it is paid, and the amount.

  • View opens Stripe’s page for that invoice.
  • PDF downloads it.

Your first invoice is usually different from the rest: it covers only the days left in the month you started, plus any one-time setup fee. After that each invoice is one full month.

If a payment does not go through

The Status tile turns red and a message says A payment did not go through. Press Change payment method, update the card, and Stripe tries again. If the card is right and it still fails, contact DTS.

If billing is not set up yet

Before your subscription starts, the page has no tiles. If DTS has sent a payment link it says Finish setting up your plan, with a Finish setting up payment button that opens the payment page.

Changing or cancelling your plan

Talk to your DTS representative. Plans cover different levels of service, so a change is agreed with DTS first and then shows here.
