Questions and help

Quick answers to the things churches ask most, and who to contact for the rest.

Signing in

I forgot my password.

Ask DTS for a password reset link. It opens a page where you choose a new password. There is no “forgot password” button on the sign-in page.

My invitation says “This link no longer works”.

It expired, was already used, or was replaced by a newer link. Ask whoever invited you to invite you again from Settings, or ask DTS.

I was signed out unexpectedly.

This usually means your password was changed somewhere else or your session ended. Sign in again.

It says “No organization access”.

Your sign-in works, but it is not attached to a church at the moment, usually because your access was removed. Ask a colleague with access to invite you again, or ask DTS.

Can two of us share one sign-in?

Please do not. Inviting a colleague takes a minute and costs nothing, and changes such as stopping the staging server are recorded under the name of whoever made them. See People with access.

Status and incidents

Bedrock says my website is not responding. Do I need to tell DTS?

No. DTS sees the same incident. Check the Overview for the latest status.

My website seems down, but Bedrock says checks are passing.

Bedrock checks your site from outside at regular intervals, so a very new problem may not show yet, and a problem with one page, or with the network in your building, will not show at all. Try the site on a phone using mobile data. If it is still down, contact DTS and say what you see.

Why is a day red in the uptime history when the site was only down for a few minutes?

The colours are strict: a day turns amber below 99.9%, which is about a minute and a half. See Uptime history.

What is a “server health alert”?

A reading on the server, such as processor, memory or disk use, crossed a limit. It often has no effect on the website. See What each kind means.

The times look wrong.

Times are shown in your church’s time zone as DTS has it on file. Check it under Settings → Organization and ask DTS to correct it if needed.

Reports, billing and staging

I do not see any reports.

Reports appear once DTS has shared the first one. If you expected one by now, ask DTS.

Can I get a report as a PDF?

Yes. Open it and press Print or save as PDF. See Printing or saving a report.

Who can see our billing?

Everyone at your church with access can see the Billing page and open Stripe’s billing page. Nobody can see the full card number, including DTS.

I do not see a Staging tab.

The switch appears only if your church has a staging site. If you expected one, ask DTS.

Can I restart or resize our production server?

No. Changes to production are made by DTS, so that they are planned around your services. Ask your DTS team.
